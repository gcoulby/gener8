# On a phone or tablet

GENER8 works with fingers too. Tap ▶ in the bar to start the audio. Most of what's on this page is about the canvas: how to look around without knocking anything, and how to pick out one thing when several overlap.

## Getting around

| | |
|---|---|
| drag with one finger | Pan. |
| pinch | Zoom. |
| tap or drag in the minimap | Jump the view there. |

A patch can fill the whole screen with fields, so one finger always pans, even when it starts on a sound or inside a field. Nothing on the canvas moves unless you have selected it first.

## Pan and select modes

On a phone or small tablet (any touch screen under 900 pixels on its short side) the bar has a mode button next to **☰**. It shows the mode you're in, and tapping it switches.

- **pan** is where you start. One finger only moves the view, and taps on the canvas do nothing, so you can't change the patch by accident. Use it to listen, look around and play live.
- **select** is for editing, with the gestures below.

The palette, the minimap, the strip and the selection bar work in both modes. A bigger tablet has the full desktop bar and no mode button, and always works as in select mode.

## Selecting and moving

In select mode:

| | |
|---|---|
| tap | Select what's on top. Tap empty space to deselect. |
| tap the selection again | Step down to what's under it. Past the bottom, it deselects. |
| drag the selection | Move it. Small wobbles of your finger are ignored, so a tap never nudges it. |
| press and hold | Pick up whatever is under your finger and move it straight away, without selecting it first. On Android the phone gives a short buzz when it's picked up. |
| drag a handle | Change size or character. Handles show on the selection and are easier to grab than with a mouse. |
| double-tap a sound | Open its internals. |
| double-tap a field | Open or close its selection bar (see below). |

## The selection strip

While something is selected, a strip sits on top of the bar. It shows the selection and everything else that was under your finger when you tapped, top first, coloured by role:

- Tap another chip to select that instead. This is the touch version of <kbd>Tab</kbd>, for when a sound sits inside several fields.
- **edit** opens the selection bar.
- **✕** deselects. With nothing selected the strip goes away.

## The selection bar

On touch the selection bar (motion, group, note, retrigger and axes; see [Motion and groups](motion.md)) stays shut until you ask for it, so it doesn't cover the canvas. Tap **edit** in the strip, or double-tap a field, to open it. It opens above the strip, across the screen. Tap **edit** again to close it.

## Adding things

Tap **+** under the logo to open the palette, then drag a sound or field onto the canvas. The palette closes and the new object is selected, ready to move. This works in pan mode too.

## The bar

The phone bar keeps what you reach for while playing: ▶, **vol**, **bpm**, the key and the mode button. Tap any of them, or **☰**, for a sheet with everything else (mixer, capture, render, patches, examples, help). See [Mixing, capture and sharing](mixing.md).
